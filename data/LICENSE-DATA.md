  The extraction is https://kaikki.org/itwiktionary/Italiano/kaikki.org-dictionary-Italiano.jsonl
- English translations come from en.wiktionary, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  The extraction is https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Italian/kaikki.org-dictionary-Italian.jsonl
- Italian example sentences come from Tatoeba, CC BY 2.0 FR. Entries keep
  positions; every sentence in data/ex/ keeps its Tatoeba id.

  CC BY-SA 4.0.
